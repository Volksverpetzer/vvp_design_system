Add `warning` and `success` color tokens (both brands, light and dark; all feedback colors are now guarded to reach WCAG AA on background and surface) and a `Callout` component in ui-web: a highlighted note inside running text with a colored stripe on the left and a light tint (`info`, `warning`, `success`, `error`, optional icon). The same look is used by vvp_app's article renderer and by the legacy WPBakery message boxes on the website.
