New colour token `surfaceAccent` (`--vvp-surface-accent`, `--vvp-color-surface-accent` / `--brand-surface-accent`): the light tint of `accent`, the way `surface` is the light tint of `primary`. Volksverpetzer light `#F5E2EC` (from design, for the donation progress bar track and the amount pills' borders), dark `#281422`; Mimikama light `#E4E7E9`, dark `#2B2D2F`.
